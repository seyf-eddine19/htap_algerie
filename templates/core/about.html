{% translate "Our Story" %}

{% translate "An association born from a deeply human commitment" %}

{% blocktranslate %} The KHADIDJA National Association for Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension (HTaP ALGERIA) was established in 2018 by its founder and first president, Mrs. Khadidja Ali Kacem. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate %} Through her dedication, Mrs. Khadidja Ali Kacem helped give a voice to those affected by pulmonary arterial hypertension, making awareness, education, and patient support a top priority. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate %} Today, the association bears her name in tribute to its founder and continues the mission she initiated in service of patients and their families. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "In memory of Mrs. Khadidja Ali Kacem" %}

{% blocktranslate %} May God have mercy on her soul, grant her forgiveness, and make Paradise her final abode. Her name, dedication, and work will remain deeply rooted in the memory of our association and all the individuals she supported. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Organization Objectives" %}

01

{% translate "Early Awareness" %}

{% translate "Introduce pulmonary hypertension and its symptoms to enable treatment from the earliest stages of the disease." %}

02

{% translate "Care & Quality of Life" %}

{% translate "Address patient needs, raise moral awareness, and train them in using essential tools like oxygen masks and cylinders." %}

03

{% translate "Access to Medication" %}

{% translate "Advocate for the consistent and sustainable supply of necessary medications to manage disease symptoms." %}

04

{% translate "Equipment & Transplants" %}

{% translate "Allocation of hospital supplies, medical/surgical equipment, and advancing heart-lung organ transplantation." %}

05

{% translate "Psychological Support" %}

{% translate "Provide specialized counseling by psychiatrists and mental health professionals to prevent worsening psychological distress." %}

06

{% translate "Training & Decentralization" %}

{% translate "Train specialized medical personnel nationwide and ensure treatments are accessible across all provinces (wilayas)." %}

07

{% translate "National Day" %}

{% translate "Organize awareness campaigns and establish a National PAH Day." %}

08

{% translate "International Cooperation" %}

{% translate "Establish partnerships with learned societies as well as national and international associations." %}

09

{% translate "Hospital Units" %}

{% translate "Establish dedicated medical units specialized in PAH management within hospital centers." %}

10

{% translate "Scientific Research" %}

{% translate "Expand research initiatives on PAH and equip medical specialists with dedicated research tools." %}

11

{% translate "Early Perinatal Screening" %}

{% translate "Promote early diagnosis during pregnancy and immediately at birth to prevent severe complications." %}
