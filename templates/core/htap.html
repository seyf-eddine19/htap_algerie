{% trans "1. What is PAH?" %}

{% trans "Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension (PAH) is a rare and severe cardiovascular disease characterized by an abnormal increase in blood pressure within the pulmonary arteries. Unlike classic systemic arterial hypertension, it specifically affects the vascular network connecting the right side of the heart to the lungs." %}

{% trans "This narrowing of the small pulmonary vessels forces the right ventricle to exert continuous increased effort, which can eventually lead to right heart failure if not managed in time." %}

{% trans "2. Symptoms & Warning Signs" %}

{% trans "Symptoms often develop gradually, which sometimes makes early diagnosis complex:" %}

{% trans "Exertional Dyspnea" %}

{% trans "Unusual shortness of breath during light physical activity or daily tasks." %}

{% trans "Chronic Fatigue" %}

{% trans "Persistent feeling of exhaustion that is not relieved by rest." %}

{% trans "Dizziness & Syncope" %}

{% trans "Lightheadedness or loss of consciousness occurring mainly during exertion." %}

{% trans "Edema" %}

{% trans "Swelling in the ankles, legs, or abdomen." %}
