{% translate "Understanding the Disease" %}

{% translate "What is Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension?" %}

{% translate "Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension (PAH) is a rare condition characterized by an abnormal increase in blood pressure within the pulmonary arteries. This elevated pressure places an increased workload on the heart, particularly affecting the right ventricle." %}

{% translate "PAH requires a specialized medical evaluation to confirm the diagnosis, identify underlying causes, and establish a personalized treatment plan." %}

{% translate "Shortness of Breath" %}

{% translate "Breathlessness, particularly during physical exertion, can be a key indicator of the condition." %}

{% translate "Persistent Fatigue" %}

{% translate "Unusual and persistent tiredness may also be experienced." %}

{% translate "Specialized Diagnosis" %}

{% translate "Diagnosis relies on targeted medical tests and often requires evaluation at a specialized center." %}
